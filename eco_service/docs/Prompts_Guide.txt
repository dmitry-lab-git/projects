1. OVERVIEW & MANAGEMENT CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This guide serves as an operational tool to extract technical architecture details, 
business logic clarifications, and deployment roadmaps from the ERP Prototype 
knowledge base. 

Any project participant (Manager, Hub Sorter, Field Collector, or Resident) can 
use these pre-formatted prompts to consult the AI Guide on the system's core 
design patterns, database structures, and scaling principles.

Step-by-Step Execution:
1. Upload the system documentation files (e.g., the open-source transfer proposal 
   and the UX/UI system architecture overview) into your AI chat interface.
2. Copy and send any of the 10 thematic prompts below to analyze or expand upon 
   the current state of development.

2. TECHNICAL CONTROLS & COMPREHENSIVE AI PROMPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The following 10 prompts are divided by functional domain and structured to 
reveal established system logic as well as guide future technical specifications:

--- DOMAIN A: ARCHITECTURE, LEDGER INTEGRITY & RBAC SECURITY ---

* Prompt 1: "Explain the overall architecture and goals of the 'Auroville 
  Eco-Service' ERP system, as well as the role of the four isolated database 
  schemas (dicts, infrastructure, security, analytics)."

* Prompt 2: "How is the material accounting of waste structured in the system? 
  Tell me about the connection between the bags tables, the bag_events event log, 
  and the accounting storefront."

* Prompt 4: "Describe the four-level Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model 
  in the system: from residents and field collectors to managers and the CEO."

* Prompt 9: "How does Event Sourcing work, and why is capturing data through 
  an append-only event log more reliable than classic state tables?"

--- DOMAIN B: ADOPTION LEVELS, ECONOMICS & SIMULATION ---

* Prompt 3: "What is the logic behind the 2-month pilot project for 10 key 
  facilities (restaurants, offices, factories, guesthouses), and how does the 
  stream simulator work?"

* Prompt 5: "What are the options for field waste labeling (from laminated tags 
  to QR codes and smart containers), and how are they selected based on 
  complexity levels?"

* Prompt 6: "How is the project's economic model (CAPEX and OPEX) structured, 
  and what are the benefits of Dmitry Lab taking on development and technical 
  support?"

--- DOMAIN C: FIELD HARDWARE, ROADMAPS & COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT ---
(Note: These prompts act as triggers to detail and expand future requirements)

* Prompt 7: "What are the technical requirements for operators' smartphones 
  to scan tags (minimum OS version, camera specifications, and wireless 
  interface support)?"

* Prompt 8: "What is the implementation plan for the pilot rollout: from the 
  preparatory phase to evaluating results and scaling across the entirety of 
  Auroville?"

* Prompt 10: "What are the development prospects for the eco-service after the 
  pilot is completed, and how can resident engagement within the community be 
  ensured using the 'from simple to complex' principle?"
